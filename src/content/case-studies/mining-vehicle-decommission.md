## Situation

A public-sector coal mining subsidiary operating in the Surguja coal belt had a holding yard of retired heavy equipment — dozers, dumpers, and Poclain excavators — some idle for years. The equipment was tendered for disposal through a formal auction process requiring a GST-registered buyer with proven heavy-dismantling capability.

## Scope

Shahin Scrap secured the lot through the auction and committed to a six-week window for on-site cutting, segregation, and complete removal, including compliance documentation for every consignment leaving the premises.

## Execution

Our cutting crews worked inside the demarcated yard with the client's safety protocols. Each vehicle was drained of fluids, stripped of non-ferrous components (copper windings, aluminium radiators, brass fittings), and cut for transport. Every truckload was weighed and documented before dispatch.

## Outcome

Fourteen heavy vehicles — roughly 320 tonnes of recoverable metal — were cleared within the committed window. The yard was handed back clean, with a full disposal record set delivered to the client's stores department.
